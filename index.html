Atlas

A private personal assistant and learning companion, built and used by one person: Thomas Southwell.

What Atlas does

  • Keeps Thomas's tasks and reminders.
  • Reads his Gmail inbox and Google Calendar to write a daily brief, sort incoming email, and prepare for meetings.
  • Drafts emails and calendar changes. Nothing is sent or changed until Thomas personally approves it.
  • Researches topics and helps him study what it has researched.

Who can use it

Atlas is not a public service. It runs on Thomas's own computer and is only ever connected to his own Google account. No one else can sign up or use it.

Privacy

How Atlas handles Google account data is set out in the privacy policy.
